FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Creativo, plans, and how it all works.

Getting Started
What is Creativo?+
Creativo is Sri Lanka's portfolio platform built for graphic designers and video editors. Upload your work, get discovered by brands and clients, and grow your creative career — all in one place.
Is it free to create an account?+
Yes! Creating an account is completely free. You can publish up to 3 projects, get a public profile page, and appear in search — no credit card needed.
How do I create an account?+
Click the "Join Free" button in the top navigation. Fill in your name, email, specialty, and a password. Your profile goes live instantly after signing up.
Can I sign back in with my password?+
Yes. Use the "Sign In" button and enter the email and password you registered with. If you forget your password, contact us via WhatsApp and we'll help you reset it.
Plans & Payments
What's the difference between the plans?+
The Free plan lets you publish up to 3 projects and appear in search. The Pro plan (LKR 2,490/mo) unlocks unlimited projects, a verified badge, full analytics, priority in search, social links, an inquiry inbox, and exclusive profile customisation features like animated name styles and profile frames.
How does payment work?+
Payment is made via bank transfer. Once you initiate an upgrade, you'll receive our bank details. After transferring, send us a WhatsApp confirmation and your Pro account will be activated within 24 hours.
Can I cancel my subscription?+
Yes. You can cancel anytime by messaging us on WhatsApp. Your Pro features will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?+
Absolutely. You can upgrade to Pro anytime from the Pricing page. To downgrade, reach out to us on WhatsApp and we'll process it before your next billing cycle.
Portfolio & Projects
How do I upload a project?+
Sign in and click the "+ Upload Project" button from your Dashboard. Add a title, category, description, and your project file (image or video). You can also add a custom thumbnail. Hit Publish and your work goes live immediately.
What file types can I upload?+
You can upload images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP) and video files (MP4, MOV). Free accounts support files up to 50 MB; Pro accounts support up to 250 MB per file.
Can I edit or delete a project after uploading?+
You can delete a project from your Dashboard at any time. Full project editing (title, description, cover) is coming soon. For urgent edits, message us on WhatsApp.
How do clients contact me?+
Your profile features a "Hire Me" button that lets clients send you a brief directly. Pro users receive these in a dedicated Inquiry Inbox inside their Dashboard, with unread notifications.
